# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful, assume good intent, critique ideas and evidence rather than people, and avoid sharing
private workplace information. Maintainers may remove abusive, discriminatory, threatening, or
privacy-violating content and restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's contact channel. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
